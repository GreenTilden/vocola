Vocola

Take any song. Rewrite the lyrics about your kids.
Family parody songs made with AI.

What is this?

You upload (or link) a song. Vocola listens, separates the vocals, sends the lyrics to an LLM with your kids' names + whatever's making them laugh today, and synthesizes new vocals over the original instrumental. Out comes a parody of a song you already know — except now it is about your kid singing cereal jingles, or whatever phase they are in this month.

Who's it for?

Parents who already make up bedtime songs by default. Families who want to immortalize their kids' weird phases in music. Anyone who thinks the best songs on the radio could use your kid's name in them.

Built on:

ElevenLabs Chatterbox TTS Demucs Ollama (local LLM) FastAPI Vue 3
